Our Heritage

More Than a Club.
A Legacy.

Since 1998, the region's premier home for championship snooker and pool.

Founded 1998

A Quarter Century
of Championship Play

"We didn't build a snooker hall. We built the kind of venue we wished had existed when we first picked up a cue."

Founded in 1998 by national semi-finalist Arthur Pemberton with six tables, The Cue Club now spans 24 championship tables across three disciplines.

Built on world-specification surfaces and welcoming club culture, six academy graduates have gone on to compete on the professional tour.

28 Years of History

The Club
Milestones

Twenty-eight years from six tables to the region's flagship cue sports arena.

1998
Foundation

Arthur Pemberton opens The Cue Club with 6 match tables.

2001
First Tournament

Inaugural Cue Club Open launches our annual tournament calendar.

2004
First Pro Alumni

Junior graduate James Hartley earns his professional tour card.

2008
Pool & Carom Wing

Added 8 American pool tables and 4 heated carom tables.

2013
Academy Launch

Structured coaching pathways launch for all player skill levels.

2017
WSF Accreditation

Awarded World Snooker Federation coaching facility accreditation.

2021
Digital Dashboard

Launched online table reservations, hours tracking, and tournament entry.

2026
Today

24 championship tables, 1,200+ members, and 18 annual tournaments.
